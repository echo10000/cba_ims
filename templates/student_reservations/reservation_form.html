Student Equipment Reservation Form

Submit a reservation request for instructional equipment. No student account or password is required. Requests are reviewed by the CBA Property Custodian.

Physical School ID Custody Policy

  • Identity Verification: Your identity will be physically verified using your original school ID upon equipment collection.
  • ID Deposit: You must leave your physical school ID in custody at the CBA Property Office while borrowing equipment.
  • Pickup Grace Period: Approved reservations are held for 15 minutes past your requested pickup time.
  • Return & ID Collection: Upon inspection at the CBA office (or next-day verification for guard returns), you will collect your deposited ID.
